**Advisor:** [Prof. Nathaniel Bleier](https://eecs.engin.umich.edu/people/bleier-nathaniel/), University of Michigan · 2026/01 – 2026/05

- Developed a formally verified Chisel-Verilog aligner that leverages Yosys and FIRRTL to map optimized Verilog signals back to the original Chisel source with signal-level accuracy.
- Resolved traceability bottlenecks in agile hardware design, outperforming ML-driven methods and enabling downstream applications such as automated HDL debugging, semi-formal verification, and AI-driven testbench generation.
